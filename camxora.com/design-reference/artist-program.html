How it works

Start creating

Make a track and publish it from your library. Keep it private until it is ready.

Get heard

Real listeners, 30 seconds or more, count as valid streams. Owners do not count.

Earnings settle after a 7-day hold. Request a payout at $5 or more.

Eligibility

Per track

1,000 valid streams

Played for at least 30 seconds, or 50% of a track shorter than 60 seconds.

100 unique listeners

Different signed-in listeners, excluding the track owner and suspicious traffic.

Verified creator

Verified email, a complete profile, and a payout method on file.

Questions

Contact the team

Do AI-generated tracks qualify?

Yes. AI-generated tracks are eligible in the first version of the program. Original uploads are planned for a later phase.

How much can I earn?

Rates are set per stream and capped per track. The full rate card is published in your creator dashboard before you apply.

When do I get paid?

Earnings settle after a 7-day hold. Request a payout at $5 or more via Wing, ABA or bank transfer.

Do I keep ownership?

You keep ownership of your work. Camxora gets a licence to stream and promote it on the platform.
