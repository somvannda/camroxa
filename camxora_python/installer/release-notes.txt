Camxora 1.5.21
YouTube upload credits + Progress publishing
November 4, 2026

What's new
----------
- Credits now show exactly what a plan costs, including YouTube uploads. The
  automation panel prices every step live (draft, Suno, image, video export,
  merge, upload) and adds a YouTube column to the plan total.
- Publish finished videos without leaving the app: right-click a completed row
  in Progress and choose "Upload to YouTube". With the YouTube Upload toggle on,
  merges publish automatically.
- Completed rows tell you where delivery stands: uploading, uploaded (with the
  video link), upload failed with a retry hint, or upload blocked.

Changed
-------
- The automation toggle is now labelled "YouTube Upload" and explains that
  uploads go through Post for Me and are billed per video.
- The credit check before generating counts upload credits too, so the button
  total and the server's required total match.

Fixed
-----
- Connecting a YouTube channel updates immediately: the Settings panel now waits
  for the browser sign-in instead of needing a manual Refresh.
- Turning the upload toggle off no longer forgets your saved preference.
- A merged file can no longer be published twice (and charged twice).

Requirements
------------
- Installer: CamxoraSetup-1.5.21.exe
- Production API: https://api.camxora.com/api/v3

Still included
--------------
- Credits-only desktop studio access: a live credit pack unlocks the studio for
  30 days; buying again resets the clock.
- Buy Credits dialog with the four web-music packs.
- Create channel profiles directly in Settings -> Profiles.
- Continue with Google on the sign-in card.
- Suno V6 model series (V6, V6 Mini, V6 Wild) plus V5.5 / V5 options.
- YouTube upload automation for connected channel profiles.
