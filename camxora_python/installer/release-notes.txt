Camxora 1.5.0
Sign in with Google
November 3, 2026

What's new
----------
- "Continue with Google" on the sign-in card. Sign in (or create your account)
  with a Google account instead of typing a password. Your browser handles the
  Google prompt and the app takes over from there; your password and tokens are
  never shared with the browser.
- Same access rules as before: the studio still requires live credit-pack
  credits, and a free account gets the usual upgrade prompt.
- Clear messages if the browser is closed, the Google prompt is cancelled, or
  another CAMXORA window is already signing in.

Requirements
------------
- Installer: CamxoraSetup-1.5.0.exe
- Production API: https://api.camxora.com/api/v3

Still included
--------------
- Credits-only desktop studio access: a live credit pack unlocks the studio for
  30 days; buying again resets the clock.
- Buy Credits dialog with the four web-music packs.
- Create channel profiles directly in Settings -> Profiles.
- Suno V6 model series (V6, V6 Mini, V6 Wild) plus V5.5 / V5 options.
- YouTube upload automation for connected channel profiles.
