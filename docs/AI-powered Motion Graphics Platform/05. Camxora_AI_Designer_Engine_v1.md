# Camxora AI Designer Engine
## Technical Design Specification
### Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

The AI Designer Engine is the intelligence layer of Camxora.

Unlike traditional AI video generators, the AI Designer never renders pixels.
Instead, it understands user intent, composes reusable motion graphics scenes,
and generates Scene JSON that is rendered by the Camxora Engine.

---

# Vision

Users should describe what they want.

Example:

> "Create a futuristic cyberpunk EDM visualizer with purple lightning, neon particles and an aggressive bass-reactive circular spectrum."

The AI should transform this request into a complete, editable Scene JSON.

---

# Core Responsibilities

The AI Designer SHALL:

- Understand natural language prompts
- Analyze existing projects
- Recommend assets
- Build Scene JSON
- Modify existing scenes
- Explain design decisions
- Optimize visual balance
- Save reusable templates

The AI SHALL NOT:

- Render frames
- Replace the rendering engine
- Produce irreversible edits

## 6. Rule Engine

Business Rules

Example

NOT compatible with

Heavy Particles

Example

Festival Theme

Requires

High Saturation

---

## 7. Scene JSON Generator

Output

Scene JSON only.

The renderer consumes the JSON.

The AI never renders graphics.

---

## 8. Validation Engine

Checks

- Missing assets
- Invalid values
- Conflicting effects
- Unsupported plugins
- Performance budget

# Design Principles

1. AI edits data, not pixels.
2. Every decision must be explainable.
3. Every generated scene remains editable.
4. AI always uses reusable components.
5. Existing templates are preferred over generating duplicates.
6. The knowledge base is the foundation of AI quality.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The AI Designer becomes a virtual senior motion graphics designer that understands creative intent, composes professional scenes from reusable components, and continuously improves as the Camxora asset library grows.
