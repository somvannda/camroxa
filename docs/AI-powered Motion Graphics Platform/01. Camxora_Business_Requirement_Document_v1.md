# Camxora Business Requirement Document (BRD)
## AI Motion Graphics Platform
### Version 1.0 (Draft)

---

# 1. Executive Summary

Camxora is an AI-native Motion Graphics Platform that enables creators to produce professional-quality animated music videos without requiring motion graphics knowledge.

Unlike traditional video editors, Camxora is built around reusable components, intelligent templates, and AI-assisted scene generation.

The platform combines:

- AI-assisted design
- Component-based rendering
- Motion graphics engine
- Asset libraries
- Reusable templates
- High-performance rendering

---

# 2. Vision

Create the world's easiest platform for building professional motion graphics videos.

Users should describe what they want.

Camxora should design it.

Users refine it.

Camxora renders it.

# 7. AI Requirements

AI SHALL:

- Understand prompts
- Build scene JSON
- Recommend assets
- Modify existing scenes
- Explain design decisions
- Save reusable templates

AI SHALL NOT:

- Directly render frames
- Replace rendering engine
- Generate duplicate templates intentionally

---

# 10. Long-Term Vision

Camxora becomes a platform where:

- AI creates scenes.
- Users customize scenes.
- Templates become reusable assets.
- Plugins extend the platform.
- Multiple AI providers can be integrated.
- The rendering engine remains independent from AI providers.

The platform should become the Figma of motion graphics creation.
