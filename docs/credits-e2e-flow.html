3 · Generate

Request

V6 / V6 Mini / V6 Wild. Price from the credit_pricing table only.

▼
Quote

10 credits per model (100,000 units). No plan lookup.

▼
free Preview cap

V6-Pro and V6-Wild: 1 each per month, 60s. V6-Mini always full.

▼
ledger Atomic debit

Wallet −10, lot decremented FIFO, credit_transactions row — one transaction.

▼
Deliverable

Full track for Pro/Mini; 60s preview for Free Pro/Wild.

4 · Refund

fail Provider fails

Generation errors, timeouts or upstream rejection.

▼
refund refund_once

Wallet +10 and the credits return to a live lot. Keyed on (user, reason, ref) so retries cannot double-refund.

▼
Preview slot released

A failed preview frees its monthly slot, not just its credits.

5 · Expiry / Reset

Daily sweep

grant_maintenance → expire_lapsed_lots + free_monthly_grant.

▼
pack 30 days pass

Pack credits lapse: wallet clawed back, lot zeroed, tier → Free.

▼
Free reset

New month: previous free lot removed, balance set back to 50. Never stacks.

▼
legacy Converted plan credits

legacy_conversion_2026_10_30 lots expire at the original plan date and are clawed back. No exception.

▼
Buy again

New pack → Pro again. Expiry re-stamped on every purchase.

Implemented

  • Free/Pro derived from live pack lots
  • Monthly 50-credit reset (free_monthly_grant)
  • 1 Pro + 1 Wild preview per month, 60s
  • V6 / V6 Mini / V6 Wild at 10 credits
  • Atomic debit + FIFO lot decrement
  • Refund restores the consumed lot
  • Every credit expires within 30 days
  • Wallet/lot invariant alert

Removed

  • Plans, licenses, subscriptions, quotas
  • Profile add-ons and allowance limits
  • V5 / V5.5 models and pricing
  • Legacy /billing/credit-pack/payments
  • Disabled /credits/purchase
  • user_portal (app.camxora.com)
  • Plan quota meters and renewal emails
