# Music web: album tracklist alignment

**Date:** 2026-08-17

## Summary

- Album track rows keep duration in the Time column and the like button in its own column.
- The Plays column is hidden until a track has at least one play.
- Album hero hides a "0 plays" stat and uses the shared collection hero layout.

- `music_web` — `track-row`, `album-track-list`, `album-hero`, `album-playback-controls`
